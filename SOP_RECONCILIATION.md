# SOP: End-of-Day Financial Reconciliation (v1.0)

## 1. Physical Drawer Verification
- Match "Cash Expected" against "Cash Actual."
- Any variance > $5.00 must be flagged as a 'Logic Gap.'

## 2. Tender Integrity
- Ensure all CARD transactions are closed as 'CARD.'
- 'OTHER' and 'CASH' overrides are prohibited for digital settlements.

## 3. Hardware Pulse
- Verify 'Cloud Icon' on Square POS is green.
- Force-sync any 'Offline Transactions' before drawer close.

**Escalation:** Contact Systems Architect (Armstrong) for all Level-Omega failures.
